Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers regarding Poshak sizing, care instructions, and order delivery.

How do I know the correct Poshak size for my Laddu Gopal idol?

Measure the height of your Laddu Gopal idol from head to toe in inches. Refer to our Size Guide Chart. For instance, an idol of 3 inches height usually requires Size 2 or 3 Poshak.

Are the mukuts and accessories included with the Poshak?

Every Heavy Velvet & Zardozi Poshak comes with a matching Patka and Pagdi/Lace. Complete 7-piece Shringar sets (Haar, Bajubandh, Kundan Payal) can be ordered separately under our Shringar section.

What is your delivery timeframe across India?

All standard orders are dispatched within 24 hours. Metro cities receive delivery in 2-3 business days, while non-metro cities take 4-5 business days. Express Janmashtami shipping is available.
